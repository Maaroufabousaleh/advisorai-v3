# Phase 6 — Institutional controls and research validity

## Objective

Complete the validity, risk, accounting, and attribution controls required for
defensible paper trading.

## Work packages

1. Compare constrained targets with no-trade, equal-weight, inverse-volatility,
   simple risk-budget, and prior champion portfolios.
2. Add robust covariance/factors, liquidity/capacity, margin, scenario/stress,
   drawdown and expected-shortfall diagnostics; RiskKernel remains the sole veto.
3. Complete OMS/reconciliation/TCA and reconcile P&L/risk/execution attribution.
4. Add purged walk-forward, multiple-testing, sensitivity, regime, model
   inventory, independent challenge, incident, and postmortem fixtures.

## Exit gate

Every paper order passes point-in-time, portfolio, cost, capacity, stress, and
hard-limit checks. Attribution reconciles exactly enough to create an incident on
an unexplained residual.

## Explicitly out of scope

No live capital, no risk-limit relaxation by any agent, and no VaR-only safety
claim.
