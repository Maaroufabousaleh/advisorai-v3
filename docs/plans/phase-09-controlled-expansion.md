# Phase 9 — Controlled expansion and Research Brain

## Objective

Implement the empirical Research Brain and elastic Alpha Team workflows as
first-class infrastructure, then admit bounded sources, capabilities,
strategies, and model challengers on their own evidence. Research foundations
may begin before Phase 7; promotion remains gated.

## Work packages

1. Implement literature provenance and empirical registries for
   `HypothesisCard`, `CandidateFactor`, `ExperimentArtifact`,
   `ValidationReport`, `PromotionDecision`, and `OutcomeMemory` with exact
   code/data/model/environment hashes.
2. Keep DuckDB/Parquet/SQLite as empirical truth. Treat vector/wiki/memory
   packages as optional retrieval. Preserve provenance from every material
   research claim to the originating source.
3. Implement Scout, Research Lead, Research Brain, Experiment Factory,
   Statistical Red Team, and outcome workflows as cancellable elastic jobs.
   Start with one coordinator and at most one or two research workers; one
   global GPU lease; research yields to trading health; nothing must run while
   AdvisorAI is shut down.
4. Record immutable decision-time inputs for eligible paper decisions and
   attach outcomes later through separate `OutcomeResolution` IDs. Do not
   train AdvisorAI-CDM in this phase plan.
5. After integrated paper evidence exists, reopen a challenger only for a
   measured deficiency in the frozen reference fabric. TimesFM 3, TTM-R3,
   Kronos-base, TabPFN-TS, Kev/Jev variants, and future AdvisorAI-CDM remain
   separately gated; model release novelty is not a trigger.
6. Qualify U.S. daily research bars and official issuer evidence through the
   [equity integration plan](equity-data-and-research-integrations.md). Evaluate
   Alpaca historical SIP and SEC EDGAR first, then a secondary daily-bar
   candidate. Admit provider adapters and optional tools one at a time. Keep
   equity broker-paper qualification separate from frozen research replay.
   Keep browser collectors and optional cloud workers behind their existing
   ports and qualification boundaries.
7. Prototype official-text-first event evidence after Integrated Alpha. Defer
   broad third-party transcript and audio ingestion until access, recording,
   storage, transformation, retention, latency, and redistribution rights are
   established. Verify material numbers against an official filing or release.
8. Benchmark Qlib on a frozen equity snapshot before running bounded RD-Agent
   search inside the Qlib adapter. Test TradingAgents on selected dated Equity
   Council cases. Use Awesome Quant as a discovery list and
   route missing research implementations through the optional
   [Hermes paper-to-code trial](paper-to-code-prototype.md).

9. Evaluate the [QuantMind paper-evidence prototype](quantmind-paper-evidence.md)
   inside the existing Research Brain. Start with one admitted, versioned
   PDF and verify the exact page and supporting passage for each finding.

## Research Brain separation

- **Knowledge/literature layer:** papers, concepts, equations, methods,
  relationships, contradictions, citations, and license/provenance. LLM Wiki
  is a candidate; Qanat is an isolated adapter candidate.
- **Empirical/scientific layer:** canonical experiment/outcome records,
  immutable artifact identities, failed/rejected evidence, and promotion
  decisions. Retrieval systems never replace this truth. Cognee, Mem0, and
  alternatives remain optional challengers.

The [optional OSS portfolio optimization bake-off](portfolio-optimization-evaluation.md)
is a later, bounded allocation research extension that compares skfolio and
CVXPY/native convex candidates against existing baselines. HiGHS and CP-SAT
are conditional problem-specific tools, not a required second optimizer stack;
no entry expands the current PAPER-V1 venue/asset scope. The Portfolio
Constructor, RiskKernel, OMS, NautilusTrader, Prefect and Resource Governor
retain their existing authority.

The [technology and integration decision register](../reference/technology-decision-register.md)
is the canonical inventory for source tiers and tool decisions. It records
implementation and admission separately. The [native integration roadmap](native-first-integration-roadmap.md)
selects read-only OpenBB, bounded Optuna search, and Qlib/RD-Agent equity
research for the full paper workstation. These tools operate behind existing
AdvisorAI contracts with research-only permissions. Cloud workers remain optional.

## Admission gate

Admit one new source, strategy, capability, model, or asset scope at a time.
Require exact identity, point-in-time validity, replayability, realistic costs,
independent review where applicable, and positive marginal evidence for the
requested scope without safety/resource regression. The gate controls
promotion and authority; a pending challenger does not block Research Brain,
experiment registry, or other quarantined implementation.

## Explicitly out of scope

No autonomous strategy activation, no self-promotion, no broker credentials in
research/sandbox processes, no live-capital authority, and no new model
comparison prerequisite for infrastructure.
